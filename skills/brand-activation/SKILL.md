# Brand Activation

Brand Activation designs physical, digital, and cultural acts that generate organic attention, cultural conversation, and real human utility beyond standard paid media.

---

## 1. The 9 Activation Formats

1. **Brand Utility**: Building a functional service, physical tool, or digital platform that solves an unaddressed consumer problem.
2. **PR Stunt / Guerilla**: An unexpected public demonstration engineered to trigger news coverage and social commentary.
3. **Cultural Hijack**: Repurposing an established cultural moment, landmark, or public ritual.
4. **Subversive Demonstration**: A provocative physical torture test or comparative experiment in public space.
5. **Interactive Installation / Ambient**: Immersive, multi-sensory physical environments inviting direct participant manipulation.
6. **Social Experiment**: Staging an authentic human situation in public to reveal and challenge social norms.
7. **Product Innovation / Packaging Hack**: Re-engineering the physical product or packaging to deliver a political or environmental statement.
8. **Digital-Physical Bridge**: Connecting physical objects to real-time generative code, AR, or digital ecosystems.
9. **Cultural Object / Artefact**: Creating an enduring, tangible artefact that outlives the marketing cycle.

---

## 2. The Non-Advertising Diagnostic Test

Before approving an activation concept, apply the diagnostic test:

> **"If we remove the advertising campaign entirely, does this object or act still possess intrinsic value and cultural meaning?"**
> - **Yes** $\rightarrow$ `non_advertising` platform (Scalable, high earned media, award-grade).
> - **No** $\rightarrow$ `execution` mechanic (Single-format tactic, dependent on media spend).

---

## 3. Activation Decision Matrix

- **High Budget / High Risk**: Physical utility or cultural infrastructure (e.g. permanent public installations).
- **Low Budget / High Originality**: Cultural hijack or packaging subversion.
- **Participation Floor**: The physical mechanic must be intuitively understandable within 3 seconds of contact.
