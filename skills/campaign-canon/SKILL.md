# Campaign Canon

Campaign Canon connects modern commercial design to the world's most rigorous library of 571 verified legendary campaigns across Cannes, D&AD, and Effie history. It serves as an anti-derivative benchmark to ensure new work is strategically grounded, structurally distinct, and not merely repeating saturated clichés.

---

## 1. Pattern Taxonomy (P01–P18)

Every canonical campaign is categorized by its primary mechanic:

| Pattern | Name | Core Mechanic | Saturation Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| `P01` | Extreme Consequence | Exaggerating the benefit to an absurd extreme | Low |
| `P02` | Inversion / Anti-Hero | Turning weakness or villain into the core proof | Medium |
| `P03` | Uncomfortable Truth | Stating the taboo or unsaid category reality | Medium |
| `P04` | Brand Utility | Solving a real human problem instead of just talking | Low |
| `P05` | Provocative Question | Re-framing common cultural assumptions | Medium |
| `P06` | Radical Transparency | Showing flaws, raw process, or internal secrets | Low |
| `P07` | Cultural Hijack | Intercepting an existing cultural event or conversation | High |
| `P08` | Product Torture Test | Pushing physical product capability to destruction | Medium |
| `P09` | Emotional Parable | Character-driven narrative demonstrating value | **Saturated (50+ cases)** |
| `P10` | Interactive / Gamified | Requiring active audience participation or code | Low |
| `P11` | Social Experiment | Staging real-world reactions to reveal human behavior | **Saturated (50+ cases)** |
| `P12` | Subversive Demonstration | Challenging competitor conventions via real demo | Medium |
| `P13` | Relatable Slice-of-Life | Capturing micro-moments of authentic daily struggle | Medium |
| `P14` | Visual Pun / Metaphor | Single-image visual synthesis delivering the claim | Medium |
| `P15` | Absurdist Comedy | Surrealistic non-sequitur logic anchoring memorability | Medium |
| `P16` | Cause / Purpose | Aligning brand power to a societal transformation | **Saturated (50+ cases)** |
| `P17` | Technology Demo | Harnessing novel tech/AI to dramatize a benefit | Low |
| `P18` | Nostalgia / Cultural Remix | Re-contextualizing historical cultural icons | Medium |

## 2. Anti-Derivative & Saturation Rules

1. **Empirical Saturation Cap**:
   - If a proposed concept matches a saturated pattern (`P09`, `P11`, `P16`) with 50+ canonical cases, cap originality score at **6.0 / 10** unless a structurally distinct variant mechanic is introduced.
2. **Cross-Category Borrowing**:
   - Recombining proven mechanics from distant categories (e.g. applying a `P04` utility mechanic from automotive into luxury fashion) is encouraged and creates high-originality work.
3. **Pre-Mortem Calibration**:
   - Before signing off on a concept, compare it against 3–5 canonical cases in the same pattern to identify failure modes before launch.
