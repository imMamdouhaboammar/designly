# Visual Storytelling

Visual Storytelling structures cinematic narrative arcs, storyboard progressions, and visual pacing to evoke precise emotional transformation in the viewer.

---

## 1. The 6 Narrative Frameworks

1. **Kenn Adams' Story Spine**:
   - *Once upon a time... Every day... Until one day... Because of that... Because of that... Until finally... And ever since then...*
2. **Nancy Duarte's Sparkline**:
   - Continuous contrast between **What Is** (current frustrating reality) and **What Could Be** (transformed future state), ending with a New Bliss.
3. **Freytag's Dramatic Pyramid**:
   - Exposition $\rightarrow$ Inciting Incident $\rightarrow$ Rising Action $\rightarrow$ Climax $\rightarrow$ Falling Action $\rightarrow$ Resolution.
4. **Monroe's Motivated Sequence**:
   - Attention $\rightarrow$ Need $\rightarrow$ Satisfaction $\rightarrow$ Visualization $\rightarrow$ Action.
5. **Pixar's Rules of Story**:
   - Character desire over comfort; admiring a character for trying rather than for succeeding; simplifying focus.
6. **The Hero's Journey (Campellian/Vogler)**:
   - Departure $\rightarrow$ Initiation $\rightarrow$ Return with the Elixir.

---

## 2. Emotional Specificity (Tier 1/2/3 Hierarchy)

Great storytelling avoids generic "positive/happy" states and targets distinct Tier 2 and Tier 3 emotional registers:

- **Tier 1 (Generic, score $\le 6$)**: Happy, sad, excited, angry.
- **Tier 2 (Specific, score 6–8)**: Nostalgic longing, defiant pride, quiet vindication, bittersweet relief.
- **Tier 3 (Complex / Dual, score 8–10)**: Bittersweet triumph, vulnerable defiance, ironic sincerity, anxious optimism.
