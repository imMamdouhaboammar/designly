# Example: Saudi Executive Event Visual

## User request

Create a premium Arabic key visual for a five-year corporate aviation anniversary in Jeddah, 4:5 social format, with exact supplied logo and Arabic headline

## Marketing lock

Objective: establish event stature and attendance confidence

Primary message: five years of shared achievement are represented through the people behind the work

Avoid: generic gold luxury, neon, aircraft silhouettes used as filler, and regional clichés unrelated to the event

## Concept

Use one real employee moment as the visual proof of the anniversary rather than turning aviation symbols into spectacle

## Structure

- one candid human group is the dominant focal event
- Arabic headline gets a deliberate quiet field shaped around the chosen portrait crop
- grid uses two strong anchors: portrait mass and headline edge
- logo remains tertiary but clearly readable
- background architecture supports place and scale without competing detail

The Arabic text can sit right, centered, or on another valid anchor depending on the final portrait and brand grid; reading flow and hierarchy decide placement, not a fixed percentage

## Craft

- warm practical venue light plus one soft neutral key
- official brand palette and observed brand geometry only
- exact Arabic typography added deterministically if the generator cannot render it reliably
- no glow, particles, aircraft ornaments, or black-and-gold shorthand unless the supplied identity explicitly calls for them

## Preflight

Pass when the one-second read is people + anniversary, text field does not fight faces, and the concept still works with all finishing effects removed

## QA

Run thumbnail, grayscale, edge, Arabic copy, brand-off, and effect-subtraction tests
