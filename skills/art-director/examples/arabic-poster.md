# Example: Arabic Typography-Heavy Poster

## User request

Create an Arabic poster with the exact headline `وقت القرار` and a supplied product image

## Route

`typography-heavy` + `director`

## Structure

- define one product focal mass and one headline mass
- choose the headline anchor from the product orientation, reading order, and available negative space
- keep the text block native RTL and aligned consistently within its own structure
- line breaks preserve meaning

Do not force the headline to the right of center by rule; use the composition that creates the clearest Arabic reading path

## Execution

Pass 1: generate the image foundation with a deliberate text field

Pass 2: render or correct `وقت القرار` exactly in a deterministic layout surface or targeted edit

## Hard gates

- exact string remains `وقت القرار`
- Arabic joining and glyph construction are correct
- no Latin-style tracking or fake kashida
- product does not move or distort during copy correction
- no duplicated text fragments
- text contrast has a verification path
