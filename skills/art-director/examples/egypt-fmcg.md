# Example: Egyptian FMCG Social Ad

## User request

Create a 4:5 social ad for a supplied snack pack aimed at young adults in Egypt

## Marketing lock

Primary message: the pack is the immediate craving cue

Do not invent cultural slang, testimonials, prices, or product claims that are not supplied

## Concept

Use the exact pack breaking through one tactile ingredient field associated with the real product, so appetite comes from texture and product truth rather than generic energy effects

## Structure

- pack occupies the dominant mass
- ingredient texture frames but does not cover the pack
- headline occupies a separate low-frequency zone
- logo and CTA remain subordinate
- eye path: pack, headline, CTA

## Craft

Use food-realistic texture, coherent shadows, restrained saturation, and product-accurate color

Avoid random confetti, fake speed lines, neon, floating emoji, and meaningless badges

## QA

Run thumbnail, product-fidelity, edge, value-hierarchy, copy, and slop tests
