# Example: Cosmetic Product Manipulation

## User request

Insert a supplied glass serum bottle into a macro wet-stone scene while keeping the bottle completely recognizable

## Concept

Make hydration physically visible through the environment reacting to the exact product, not through floating sparkles or glowing liquid

## Structure

- bottle is the only primary focal point
- stone line and water movement direct attention back to the label
- background remains lower frequency than the bottle
- crop preserves full dropper and product silhouette

## Manipulation contract

- lock bottle silhouette, dropper, label, logo, liquid color, and glass proportions
- place bottle on dark wet stone with one clear contact region
- use a broad upper-left key and coherent contact/cast shadow
- reflection in stone follows bottle orientation and surface roughness
- water interacts with the base without covering the label
- glass transmits and distorts background according to thickness and curvature

## Anti-slop

No sparkles, smoke, glowing serum, random flowers, chrome ribbons, or floating 3D objects unless one becomes part of a revised concept with an explicit job

## QA

Check product fidelity, perspective, glass refraction, contact, reflection, water response, edge integration, and label readability

If only base contact fails, use `local-edit` rather than regenerate
