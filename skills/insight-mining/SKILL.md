# Insight Mining

Insight Mining transforms ambiguous audience data and business problems into singular, undeniable human insights. Great advertising does not begin with an execution; it begins with an insight that refreshes the viewer's understanding of their own experience.

---

## 1. Core Workflow

1. **Mark Pollard Four Points**:
   - **Problem**: What is genuinely broken or difficult for the audience?
   - **Insight**: The unstated human or cultural truth behind the problem.
   - **Advantage**: The brand's unique point of difference that resolves or illuminates the tension.
   - **Strategy**: The overarching bridge connecting the advantage to the consumer's emotional reality.

2. **Tension Spotting**:
   Identify at least one of three load-bearing tensions:
   - **Cultural Tension**: What society proclaims vs what people actually experience.
   - **Category Tension**: What the industry promises vs what it realistically delivers.
   - **Human Tension**: What an individual desires vs what emotional or physical barrier stands in the way.

3. **Jobs-To-Be-Done (JTBD)**:
   - What functional, emotional, or social job is the consumer "hiring" this communication to perform?

4. **Abstraction Laddering & HMW Formulations**:
   - Create 3 "How Might We" formulations across abstraction levels:
     - Broad (macro cultural challenge)
     - Medium (category behavioral shift)
     - Narrow (immediate tactical reaction)

5. **Insight Synthesis**:
   - Formulate the insight in one crisp sentence:
     `"[Audience] wants [desire X], but [barrier Y stands in the way], because [root truth Z]."`

---

## 2. Quality & Validation Gates

- **The Refresh Test**: When the target audience hears this insight, do they say: *"Yes, exactly, but I've never put it into words before"*?
- **Not a Demographic Stat**: Demographics are facts, not insights. An insight is an unspoken emotional truth.
- **Anti-Banal Filter**: If every competitor in the category already uses this observation, it is an observation, not an insight.
